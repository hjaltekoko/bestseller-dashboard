Definitions
Zone of tolerance
The highest premium over the cheapest competitor that we assume a shopper accepts without leaving the product page. Set it with the slider; the default is 5%.
Compared products
The brand's PriceShape products that have a competitor price (vendor 1). Only these can be judged against the zone.
Within zone, share within zone
Compared products whose price is at most the zone above the cheapest competitor, or below it. The share divides by compared products, or by all PriceShape products when the checkbox is on.
Cheaper than market
Compared products where our price is strictly below the cheapest competitor. The same as the zone at 0%.
Median premium
The middle premium over the cheapest competitor among the products where we are dearer, the same figure as on the Brand Coverage Map. Products where we are cheaper or at the same price are left out, so a brand that is never dearer shows a dash.
Bestsellers compared, bestsellers within zone
In-stock bestsellers from Google's lists matched to a PriceShape product by GTIN and having a competitor price. When a bestseller has several variants, its best (lowest) premium is used. Share divides by bestsellers compared.
Index, not on permanent sale / Index, permanent sale
The market index split by the brand's current discounting: products discounted less than 30% today (the ones a campaign would move) and products discounted 30% or more (treated as permanent sale). Hover the cell for the product counts behind each. The two recombine to the brand's overall market index.
Other columns
Coverage, market index, campaign status, discount coverage and permanent-sale share are the same figures as on the Brand Coverage Map, from this morning's run.
